Abstract editorial book-cover artwork, refined and quiet, for a graduate monograph on mathematics and machine learning. Flat vector-like rendering with subtle depth, restrained palette of deep navy blue (hex 123060), steel blue (hex 2a6f97), muted crimson (hex 7d202d) and warm off-white. Composition: the inverse reading of the same idea, for a back cover. A sparse regular grid of small dots at the lower left that, moving upward and to the right, merges into an increasingly smooth continuous curve, until at the top it is a single unbroken line. Points becoming a function. Dots in deep navy blue (hex 123060), the emerging curve in steel blue (hex 2a6f97) with one short muted crimson (hex 7d202d) segment near the middle. Background warm off-white, very quiet, most of the canvas empty so body text can sit on it. Editorial, minimal, calm.
